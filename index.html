How it works
Up and running in 3 minutes
1

Set up your studio profile

Add your studio name, phone, email, address, and GSTIN in Settings. This appears on every PDF automatically.

2

Create a quotation

Enter the client name, event date, venue, and your line items. GST is calculated automatically. See the PDF preview live as you type.

3

Send on WhatsApp

Hit "Share on WhatsApp" — a message with the quote summary opens in WhatsApp. Client sees a professional summary instantly.

4

Convert to invoice and track payment

When the client confirms, convert the quote to an invoice in one click. Track advance, pre-event, and final payment separately.
