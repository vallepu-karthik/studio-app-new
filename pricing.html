Questions

What happens to my data after the free trial?
All your quotes, invoices and clients are saved permanently. After the trial limit you can still view and export everything — you just can't create new documents until you upgrade.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from Stripe's billing portal anytime. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your billing period, then revert to Free.
Is my payment secure?
Payments are processed by Stripe — we never store your card details. Stripe is used by millions of businesses and is PCI DSS Level 1 certified.
Do you offer a yearly plan?
Yes — yearly billing saves you 2 months (₹9,999/year). Contact support or check back soon for the yearly option in this page.
